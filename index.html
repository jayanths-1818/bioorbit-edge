Deep Space Physiological Stressors

1. Cephalad Fluid Redistribution & SANS: In microgravity, ~2 liters of bodily fluid shifts toward the head, elevating intracranial pressure and causing optic disc edema (Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome).

2. Cardiovascular Deconditioning: The heart requires less force to pump blood without gravity, leading to myocardial atrophy, reduced stroke volume, and severe orthostatic intolerance upon planetary landing.

3. Space Radiation Risks: Deep space astronauts are exposed to Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) and unpredictable Solar Particle Events (SPE), elevating acute radiation sickness and carcinogenic risks.

Software Architecture & Standards

• NASA ExMC Ontology: The Clinical Decision Support System uses diagnostic trees and medical formulary lists compliant with NASA Exploration Medical Capability requirements.

• Edge-Native ONNX Inference: Lightweight neural models run directly on rad-hardened spacecraft compute cores with sub-20ms inference latency.

• Delay-Tolerant Networking (RFC 5050): Asynchronous bundle protocol queues telemetry packets until line-of-sight communication with NASA Deep Space Network (DSN) is restored.
